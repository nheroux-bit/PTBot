# QC Criteria

Check every precedent transaction report for:

1. **Multiples requirement** — every included transaction must include at least one explicitly stated or reliably computable valuation multiple.
2. **Geographic accuracy** — target company must fit `{geography}` unless clearly labeled as a near-miss.
3. **Date accuracy** — announcement or close date must fall between `{start_date}` and `{end_date}` unless clearly labeled as a near-miss.
4. **Sector accuracy** — target business must fit `{sector}`.
5. **Source attribution** — every financial figure and multiple must include a source URL or filing reference.
6. **Consistency** — target, acquirer, date, deal value, and multiples must match across sections.
7. **Benchmarking context** — report must compare deal multiples against relevant sector-wide M&A benchmarks.
